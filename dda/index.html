Divorce Discovery Assistant · for Mac · Beta

The financial record of a divorce, organized and verified, on your own Mac.

Divorce Discovery Assistant reads every bank, card and brokerage statement, shows what's missing month by month, and links every figure to the page it came from. Work that takes weeks by hand takes hours, and nothing leaves your Mac.

What it does

Reads every statementPoint it at a folder, a drive or a box of scans. Divorce Discovery Assistant sorts and de-duplicates thousands of files and reads statements from major banks, card issuers and brokerages.
Shows what's missingEvery account, month by month: received, missing, requested, or not due yet.
Tracks each productionWhen new documents arrive, see what changed and which gaps they filled.
Account covers and key datesA cover page for each account, and balances on the dates that matter, each linked to its statement.
Never guessesEvery figure traces to its page. When Divorce Discovery Assistant can't read something, it says UNKNOWN instead of estimating.
Locked, verifiable packagesHand the record to an attorney, the court or a client as an encrypted package. The receiving copy checks every file.

Private by design

Stays on your MacCase files and findings are kept on your Mac, encrypted, behind your own password.
No AI calls, no uploadsDivorce Discovery Assistant doesn't send your documents to any AI service or server to read them.
AccountableEvery change is recorded: who, what and when.

Who it's for

Family-law offices

Bring in a client's documents, see the whole financial picture and what's still outstanding, and keep it current as each production arrives. No per-page fees.

People going through a divorce

Organize your own statements at home before your attorney spends an hour on them, then hand over one locked package.

System requirements

  • A Mac with Apple silicon or Intel
  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  • Under 50 MB for the app, plus room for each case's copy of its documents (about the size of the documents themselves)
  • No internet connection needed. Divorce Discovery Assistant works entirely on your Mac.
  • Coming: sync between your own Macs through your iCloud account, or through a folder you choose.
