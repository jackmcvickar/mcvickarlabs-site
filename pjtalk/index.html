PJ Talk · iPhone · Mac · Coming soon to beta

A private place for just the two of you.

PJ Talk is a messaging app for one special person. Send videos, voice messages, photos and texts that stay between the two of you, kept in your own iCloud with no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It's built for long, real conversations, not group chats.

What it does

Video and voice first.Record a video or a voice message in a tap. Long recordings are sent in parts as you go, so the other person can start watching or listening before you've finished.
Words for every voice message.Voice messages and videos come with a written transcript, made right on your device, so you can read along or find what was said later.
Keep what matters.Star your favorites and heart the most special ones. Hearted photos and videos stay on your phone, ready to open anytime.
Know it got there.See when a message is sent, delivered and read or played. If something doesn't make it, PJ Talk notices and sends it again.
On iPhone and Mac.Pick up the conversation on your Mac, with your whole history in one place.

Private by design

  • Your conversation lives in your own iCloud, shared only with the one person you invite. There's no PJ Talk server and no account to create.
  • Transcripts are made on your device. Your words aren't sent anywhere to be processed.
  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics.
  • You decide what the other person sees: read receipts, delivered marks and "last active" are each your choice. An optional app lock uses its own PIN or Face ID, not your phone's passcode.

System requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
  • Mac with macOS 15 or later.
  • Both people need an Apple Account signed in to iCloud.
